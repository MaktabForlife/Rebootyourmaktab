About the Academy

Umm Abbad Academy brings Islamic learning into the daily lives of Muslim women. Its courses are designed to build a deeper connection with the Qur’an while allowing learners to study around work and family responsibilities.

Practical rollout

1

Review the current login

Inspect the signed-in academy pages and identify the existing course platform and account records.

2

Link identities and roles

Map each academy person to the learning areas they may enter, including Reboot’s legacy records.

3

Connect every scope

Populate the entrance from live Academy and Maktab enrolments, then show each person their permitted learning.

4

Pilot the handoff

Test learner and staff journeys across Reboot, Aalimiya, and a subject course before broader rollout.

The current Maktab V105.4 package is development work. Its access matrix is setup only; existing course and Reboot access records remain authoritative. The student library and wider Academy integration are still planned.
